Title: Dose-Volume Histograms in Radiotherapy
Source: IAEA - Optimization in External Beam Radiotherapy
URL: https://www.iaea.org/resources/rpop/health-professionals/radiotherapy/external-beam-radiotherapy/optimization

A dose-volume histogram, abbreviated DVH, summarizes how radiation dose is distributed across a contoured anatomical volume. It is used to evaluate dose coverage of a planning target volume and dose exposure of organs at risk.

For a target volume, a DVH can indicate the proportion of the volume receiving at least a specified dose. For an organ at risk, it can support the assessment of whether dose-volume constraints are met. 

DVHs are useful for comparing alternative treatment plans, but they do not contain spatial information. A DVH alone cannot show which anatomical region within an organ receives a high or low dose. Therefore, DVHs should be interpreted together with the three-dimensional dose distribution and clinical context.

This document is an educational technical summary for a RAG demonstration. It is not intended for clinical treatment decisions.
