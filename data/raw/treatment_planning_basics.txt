Title: External Beam Radiotherapy Planning Basics
Source: IAEA - Radiotherapy Process
URL: https://www.iaea.org/resources/hhc/medical-physics/radiotherapy/process

External beam radiotherapy treatment planning aims to deliver a prescribed dose to a defined target volume while reducing exposure of surrounding healthy tissue and organs at risk.

Modern planning is commonly based on CT imaging. CT data supports the delineation of target volumes and organs at risk. It also provides electron-density information that can be used by dose calculation algorithms to account for differences between tissue types.

A treatment plan typically includes imaging, contouring, beam configuration, dose calculation, plan evaluation, verification, and clinical approval. Dose distributions can be reviewed visually with isodose lines and quantitatively with dose-volume histograms.

This document is an educational technical summary for a RAG documentation. It is not intended for clinical treatment decisions.
